## Inspiration (no text reused)

The `zeno` loop's method skills were inspired by the discipline of **superpowers**
(`github.com/obra/superpowers`, MIT © 2025 Jesse Vincent), and goal-backward verification came as a
concept from **get-shit-done** (`github.com/gsd-build/get-shit-done`). Neither was copied from, so no
license obligation attaches; the credit is intellectual, not legal. The Scope question format was adapted
in idea from `grill-me`/`grilling` (`github.com/mattpocock/skills`, MIT).
